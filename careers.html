The standard

High ownership. Low theater.

The work can be intense. The team should not be chaotic. We care about the outcome, not performative exhaustion.

You will thrive here if
  • 01You turn ambiguity into a first useful version.
  • 02You care about craft when nobody is watching.
  • 03You communicate before a deadline becomes a surprise.
  • 04You can defend your thinking without becoming precious.
  • 05You want your judgment to be visible in the final work.
Do not apply if
  • 01You need every task fully specified before you move.
  • 02You disappear when the work becomes unclear.
  • 03You defend generic output by pointing to the effort.
  • 04You avoid hard feedback or hard conversations.
  • 05You want resume decoration more than real responsibility.
Our hiring question “Would we trust this person with an important, ambiguous problem when nobody has time to babysit the process?”

Before you apply

Useful answers. No HR fog.

If your question is not here, use the final field in the application.

Are these all active full-time openings?+

No. This is an evergreen talent door. We use it for active roles, freelance work, internships, and people we may want to work with later. Current priority is creative and operations talent.

Can freshers or students apply?+

Yes. Your degree and years of experience matter less than proof, learning speed, communication, and the decisions behind your work.

What if I do not have a polished portfolio?+

Make a simple public folder, Notion page, Drive link, or GitHub profile. Show a few relevant pieces and explain what you did. We need evidence, not a fancy wrapper.

Do you hire developers?+

Rarely. We already build aggressively with AI and internal systems. Apply if you have unusual product judgment, automation depth, or a system with real users.

Can I use AI in the application?+

Yes. Use the tools you would use at work. We judge your choices and your thinking. Generic answers are easy to spot.

What happens after I apply?+

If your work and judgment fit what we need, we will contact you. Do not send repeated follow-ups. One useful update or a much better work sample is always welcome.
