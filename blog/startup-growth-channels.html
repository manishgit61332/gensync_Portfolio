Startup Growth Channels YC Founders Recommend

April 2026 • 13 min read • By Manish

We ask YC founders one question: "What's working right now for growth?" Here's what they actually say—not what Medium articles say.

#1: Founder-Led Outbound (Every.Single.One)

YC founders still do their own outbound. Not forever—but at the beginning. Personal emails to their network, warm introductions, and targeted outreach.

#2: Referral Programs

  • Dropbox: Referrals drove 35% of early growth
  • Notion: Every customer gets an ask
  • Loom: Built viral loops into product

#3: Community-First

  • Craft a community around your users
  • Slack groups, Discord, forums
  • Customers become advocates

#4: Product-Led Growth

  • Frictionless onboarding
  • Built-in share features
  • Value before asking for anything

#5: SEO & Content

  • Answer questions your users ask
  • Long-term compounding
  • High-LTV channels once established

#6: Events (Specifically)

  • Small, targeted meetups
  • Demo days
  • Industry conferences (not large ones)

The Pattern

Every growth channel YC founders share one thing: they're all focused on one-to-one. Not mass marketing. Personal, relationship-driven growth.
