Cold Outreach That Converts (Not Generic Templates)

April 2026 • 10 min read • By Manish

99% of cold emails fail. Not because cold outreach doesn't work—but because people send generic garbage hoping for a miracle.

Here's the truth: personalized, specific, helpful emails still convert at 15-25%. The problem is nobody writes them.

Why Generic Emails Fail

  • "I came across your company..." — You're lying. You scraped a list.
  • "Would love to chat..." — About what? You're not specific.
  • Template everywhere — Everyone recognizes a template.
  • No specific value — Why should they respond?

The Framework That Works

We've sent thousands of cold emails. Here's what actually converts:

  1. Open with something specific about THEM. Not "I came across"—"I saw your recent launch of X" or "Your team just raised — congrats."
  2. State the specific problem you solve for them. Not "help with marketing"—"we help Series A startups get their first 50 customers."
  3. Show proof with similar companies. "We helped [Similar Company] do X." Social proof beats claims.
  4. Make asking easy. Not "let's chat"—"want me to send over a quick case study?" or "quick call?" Be specific.
  5. Follow up 3-4 times. Most people stop at one email. Follow-up is where deals happen.

Email Template (Customize This)

Subject: Quick question about [Company]'s [Recent News]

Hey [Name],

Quick context — I noticed [specific thing about their company: recent funding, new product, team growth].

We work with [similar companies] to [specific result]. Most see [metric] in [timeframe].

Worth a quick chat? I can share what's working for companies like yours.

— [Your Name]

That's it. No gimmicks. No fake urgency. Just specific, helpful, personalized outreach.

The Follow-Up Sequence

  • Email 1: Initial outreach (see above)
  • Email 2 (3 days later): "Hey [Name], just following up..."
  • Email 3 (7 days later): "Quick bump — did this make sense?"
  • Email 4 (14 days later): "Checking one last time. If timing isn't right, no worries."

What to Expect

With quality outreach:

  • 15-25% response rate (not the 1-3% you're used to)
  • 3-5% meeting conversion
  • Real conversations, not crickets
