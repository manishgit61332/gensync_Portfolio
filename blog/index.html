Frequently Asked Questions

How do startups get their first customers?

Outbound outreach to your ICP remains the highest ROI channel. Personalized emails to 50-100 targeted companies typically yields 3-5% response rate. Combined with referral programs and content marketing, startups can generate 10+ customers within 30-60 days.

What's the best marketing strategy for startups in 2026?

Founder-led outbound + content for SEO + personal branding on LinkedIn. Pick 1-2 channels and go deep. Paid ads only after unit economics work. Focus on channels that match your ICP and stage.

How much does startup marketing cost?

Startups can start with $2-5K/month for ongoing marketing. Project-based work (websites, branding, video) ranges from $5K-50K depending on scope. The key is focus—not spending more, but executing consistently.
